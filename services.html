FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a mobile or full-stack build take?

A standard mobile MVP takes 1–3 weeks. A full-stack platform typically runs 2–6 weeks, depending on feature scope, integrations, and complexity.

Do you work from Figma or product requirements?

Yes. I build from Figma, wireframes, references, or written specs. If design isn't ready, I can still structure the product cleanly and keep it easy to extend later.

Will my app work well across all devices?

Every build is tested across common screen sizes and device contexts to ensure consistent layouts, readable content, and smooth interactions.

Do you provide revisions after delivery?

Yes. Revision rounds are included for UI polish and usability updates so the final result matches your goals exactly.

Can you improve an existing app instead of building from scratch?

Absolutely. I handle UX revamps, performance work, API cleanup, and feature expansion on existing products — not just greenfield builds.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing is scoped after reviewing your goals and timeline. Send your brief and I'll reply with a clear quote within 24 hours.
